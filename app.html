FloatLens

Type a decimal number. See the exact value a 64-bit or 32-bit float stores, its bits, the rounding error, the neighbors on either side, and the shortest text that reads back the same. Then add, subtract, multiply or divide two numbers and see the real error.

Arithmetic

The decimal text is converted exactly with big-integer arithmetic and rounded to the nearest representable value, ties to even. That is what the IEEE 754 standard asks of a correct conversion, so it can differ from code that converts through a double first (for float32 that double rounding is rare but real). Arithmetic first reads both numbers as stored floats, then rounds the exact result once, which is what IEEE addition, subtraction, multiplication and division do. Not covered: NaN and infinity as inputs, other rounding modes, fused multiply-add, and the decimal formats. Runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.
